# Technical Writing Samples

Writing samples by **Brittany Nation**, created as portfolio pieces to demonstrate technical documentation skills.

Each sample was written from my own work as a self-taught email developer. The subject matter is real: these documents explain the tools, templates, and problems I work with every week. The samples themselves are portfolio pieces, written to show how I structure, explain, and troubleshoot technical information for a reader.

## My approach

- **Write for the reader's task, not the feature list.** Every document opens with what the reader is trying to do and what they need before they start.
- **One action per step.** Numbered steps stay short, in order, and testable. If a step can't be verified, it doesn't belong in the procedure.
- **Explain the why once, then get out of the way.** Readers need enough context to adapt when their situation differs from the example, and no more.
- **Troubleshooting is part of the document, not an afterthought.** If I know where readers get stuck, that information goes in the guide.
